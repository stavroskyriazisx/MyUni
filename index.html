MyUni

Choose your electives with data, not guesswork.

MyUni looks at your past exam results and what you enjoy, predicts how well you would do in each elective, and ranks them for you.

  • 1Tell us your year, your interests and how much work you want
  • 2Enter your past exam results
  • 3See a predicted grade and a ranked list of electives

Takes about a minute. Example data is already filled in, so you can just tap through. This is a demo: all predictions are examples.
